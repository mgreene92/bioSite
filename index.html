Background

PJ is a hardworking, goal-oriented family man who values honesty, integrity, and self-improvement. As the oldest of four brothers, he learned responsibility early on through sports, competition, and his father's guidance, who always encouraged him to pursue any goal with dedication and effort.

Friends and family describe PJ as intelligent, Knowledgeable, dependable, and always willing to help others. He approaches life with determination while maintaining a great sense of humor.

Becoming a father changed his perspective on life and strengthened his desire to continue growing personally and professionally.
